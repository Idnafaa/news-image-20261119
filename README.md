One image summing up Thursday 19 November 2026's world news, drawn as a
broadsheet front page: nine feature stories with drawn scenes, a figures
strip, a dotted world map with numbered pins, and the rest of the day's
news in two columns. 30 stories, 6 continents, one page.

- `render.py` draws `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL + DejaVu fonts, no network)
- `main.py` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
